Steady talks in short, calm sentences that end in a full stop. The logo does too, and its full stop is a marked day.

  • Calm and honest. Lowercase, even strokes, nothing shouting.
  • Private. No pill, cross or brain: on a home screen it reads as a name, not a diagnosis.
  • One small mark. The square full stop is the block you fill on the streak path, one a day.
  • Drawn, not typed. Every letter is built from one 18-unit stroke and true circles; the s is drawn to match.
